GapHunter

Cross-venue arbitrage where the contract is the bot —
no keeper, no cron, no off-chain anything.
Two Uniswap V2 venues on Base Sepolia drift apart. A reactive contract on Lasna watches both — and closes the gap itself, in about a second.
How it works

Lasna sits in the middle

BASE SEPOLIA

Two V2 venues

Same GTA/GTB pair, two factories, 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 seeded in each pool.

REACTIVE · LASNA

GapHunterReactive

Subscribed to both pools' Sync events. Reprices on every event, checks the spread against a 1.5% threshold, sizes the trade from live depth, estimates profit before signalling.

BASE SEPOLIA

GapHunterExecutor

Receives the callback, re-verifies profitability against live reserves, buys cheap, sells expensive, keeps the spread.

Classic keeper bots poll off-chain and race on gas. GapHunter removes the bot entirely: the price event itself triggers the trade, through Reactive callbacks.
