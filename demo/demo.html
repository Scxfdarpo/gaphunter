Cross-venue arbitrage where the contract is the bot —

Two Uniswap V2 venues drift apart. A reactive contract on Lasna watches both — and closes the gap itself, in about a second.
How it works

Lasna sits in the middle — of two chains

BASE SEPOLIA + UNICHAIN SEPOLIA

Two V2 venues per chain

Same GTA/GTB pair, two factories, 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 seeded in each pool — deployed identically on both origin chains.

REACTIVE · LASNA

GapHunterReactive ×2

One reactive contract per origin chain, subscribed to both pools' Sync events. Reprices on every event, checks the spread against a 1.5% threshold, sizes the trade from live depth, estimates profit before signalling.

ORIGIN CHAIN

GapHunterExecutor ×2

Receives the callback, re-verifies profitability against live reserves, buys cheap, sells expensive, keeps the spread. Reverts if the gap is gone.

Classic keeper bots poll off-chain and race on gas. GapHunter removes the bot entirely: the price event itself triggers the trade, through Reactive callbacks.
